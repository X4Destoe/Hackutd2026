# Lifeline

**From grid failure to front door, before it spreads.** HackUTD 2026, built for the **State Farm**, **CBRE** and **Photon** tracks.

Lifeline connects fragmented city and property data (power grid, water pumps, homes, commercial buildings, leases,
generators) into one live dependency map. When a disaster hits (our demo is a Texas winter storm in Dallas), it:

- **Predicts the cascade:** which feeders, pumps, homes and buildings fail next, when, and why.
- **Reaches every affected State Farm customer through Photon** (text, iMessage, voice), understands replies in any
  language with AI, scores urgency, and **routes each person to the right help**: the nearest open warming center,
  State Farm claim site, hotel or repair vendor. Nobody is overlooked: no reply means a call, then a human callback list.
- **Sends the right specialist adjuster**, who accepts or passes from their phone (human in the loop).
- **Acts for CBRE buildings:** countdowns, dollar exposure, AI action plans that execute on approval, and safe buildings
  turned into help sites for the community.
