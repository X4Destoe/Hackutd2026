# Operation Storm: from warning to action

Operation Storm connects a property's modeled disaster impact to an executable response. A manager selects a building, compares the cost and avoided loss of a plan, approves it, and follows technician completion. Residents can request help by text and receive a safe, available destination when the simulation permits one.

## CBRE

Demonstrate Hurricane Harvey, select Bayou Plaza, and approve a ranked plan. The same approval creates work orders and tenant notices; technician replies update the work order. The executive report summarizes the resulting financial position. The product connects decisions to execution rather than stopping at a dashboard.

## Photon

Show the simulator conversations, technician DONE/BLOCKED replies, resident routing, and explicit customer-notice preview/send flow. A Photon bridge adapter and signed inbound webhook are implemented. The recorded demo uses simulated messages: real-device delivery is a separate credentialed rehearsal, not a completed claim.

## State Farm

Show a resident request, safety and capacity checks, and the chosen help site. Activate an eligible building as a community help site and show the resulting capacity. Residents can also receive an available modeled State Farm claims-desk contact, phone, and map link. Waiting shelter requests are placed automatically when a safe site opens. This demonstrates disaster assistance and recovery coordination; it does not submit an insurance claim or promise coverage.

## What the estimates mean

Historical presets use real event context with synthetic buildings, illustrative financial assumptions, and modeled hazards. Custom simulations are hypothetical. Live alerts, forecasts, and gauges are source-qualified proxies, not observed building flooding or verified power. Stale or missing live inputs suppress financial estimates and automated site eligibility. Emergency guidance and real-world site availability require authoritative verification.
